How to host trivia on this board

You are the host. Ask questions out loud, judge answers, keep score, and open this page's URL with query parameters to update the car screen. Open a new URL for every step.

  • st: screen, one of ask, reveal, score, end
  • t: category, e.g. Space
  • q: question text
  • c: up to 4 choices separated by |
  • a: correct choice letter (A-D), only on reveal
  • r: name of who answered correctly, or none
  • n and of: question number and total
  • p: up to 6 players and scores, e.g. Joe:200,Sam:100

Ask: ?st=ask&t=Space&n=1&of=5&q=Which+planet+has+the+most+moons&c=Jupiter|Saturn|Uranus|Neptune&p=Joe:0,Sam:0

Reveal: same URL with st=reveal&a=B&r=Sam&p=Joe:0,Sam:100

Finish: ?st=end&p=Joe:200,Sam:300
