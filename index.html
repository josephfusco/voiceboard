How to host games on Voiceboard

You are the host. Talk to the players, judge answers, and open this page's URL with query parameters to update the car screen. Open a new URL for every step. Players are remembered between URLs, so after the first step you only need to send score changes.

Every game

  • g: game, trivia or jeopardy
  • st: screen (see each game)
  • t: title or category
  • p: up to 6 players with scores, sets the roster, e.g. Joe:0,Sam:0
  • add: score changes, e.g. Sam:100 or Joe:-200
  • timer: seconds for a countdown, e.g. 15
  • fx: confetti

Trivia (g=trivia)

  • st: ask, reveal, score, end
  • q: question; c: up to 4 choices separated by |
  • a: correct choice letter, only on reveal; r: who got it, or none
  • n and of: question number and total

?g=trivia&st=ask&t=Space&n=1&of=5&q=Which+planet+has+the+most+moons&c=Jupiter|Saturn|Uranus|Neptune&p=Joe:0,Sam:0&timer=15

?g=trivia&st=reveal&t=Space&n=1&of=5&q=Which+planet+has+the+most+moons&c=Jupiter|Saturn|Uranus|Neptune&a=B&r=Sam&add=Sam:100

Jeopardy (g=jeopardy)

  • st: board, clue, reveal, final, end
  • cats: up to 6 categories separated by |
  • u: used clues as column letter + row number, e.g. A1,C3
  • at: the chosen clue, e.g. B3; dd=1 for a daily double
  • q: clue; a: correct response, only on reveal; r: who got it
  • w: final round wagers, e.g. Joe:500,Sam:1200

?g=jeopardy&st=board&cats=Space|Rivers|Movies|Food|Sports|Words&u=A1,C3&p=Joe:0,Sam:0

?g=jeopardy&st=clue&cats=Space|Rivers|Movies|Food|Sports|Words&at=B3&q=This+river+flows+through+Cairo&timer=10

Finish any game with st=end&fx=confetti.
