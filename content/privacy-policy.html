Effective October 3, 2026. This page explains what Voiceboard collects and where it goes. The short version: the game board keeps its data in your browser, and we don't run ads, analytics, or tracking.

What stays in your browser

The board saves a few things in your browser's local storage so a game can continue from one screen to the next:

  • player names and scores that your voice assistant put in the game's URLs
  • the winners of your last 20 finished games, shown in the Hall of Fame
  • your theme and language choices

The board does not send this data to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, and a game URL with reset=1 clears the saved players.

What reaches our server

Every screen is a web address, so whatever your assistant puts in that address (player names, questions, answers) is part of the request your browser makes to this site. Like any website, our hosting provider's servers record requests, including the address, your IP address, your browser type, and the time, and keep these logs for a limited period for security and operations. Use first names or nicknames for players if you'd rather not include full names.

Cookies

Playing sets no cookies. WordPress sets cookies only for people who log in to manage the site, and its activity features record what logged-in administrators are doing in the dashboard, not visitors.

Your voice assistant

We never receive audio. Your voice and your conversation are handled by your assistant's provider under its own privacy policy; we see only the web addresses it opens.

Children

The games are made for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and we don't ask anyone to create an account.

Changes

We plan to add optional game sessions that store a short transcript of names, scores, and questions on this site so an assistant can read the game back, plus a list of which game boards are connected right now. We'll update this policy before either goes live, and we'll change the effective date above whenever the policy changes.
