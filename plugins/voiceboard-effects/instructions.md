## Effects (any game)

- timer: seconds for an on-screen countdown, e.g. timer=15
- fx=confetti: celebrate, e.g. on the final screen
- auto: let the board move on by itself so you talk less. auto=reveal on a question with timer= and the answer in a= shows the answer when the timer ends. auto=score shows the scoreboard after a reveal; auto=next gives the next player in the roster their turn. after=SECONDS sets the delay (default: the timer, or 6). Score changes (add=) are never repeated.
